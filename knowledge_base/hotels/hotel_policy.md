# Hotel Booking Policy
category: hotels
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## Booking a hotel
Hotels offered through this assistant are partner hotels in the price tiers Midscale, Upper Midscale, Upscale, Upper Upscale and Luxury. A hotel stay is only confirmed after the booking has been completed and confirmed by the customer.

## Check-in and check-out
Standard check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is until 11:00 local time. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability and may be charged by the hotel.

## Changing a hotel booking
Check-in and check-out dates can be changed free of charge up to 24 hours before the original check-in date, subject to availability. Rate differences apply.

## Cancellation
Standard rate bookings can be cancelled free of charge until 24 hours before the check-in date. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged one night. Non-refundable (advance purchase) rates cannot be cancelled or changed.

## Payment
Unless stated otherwise, payment is made at the hotel. City and tourist taxes are not included in the room rate and are payable at the hotel.

## Special requests
Requests such as adjoining rooms, accessible rooms, extra beds or cots are passed on to the hotel but cannot be guaranteed until confirmed by the hotel.
