# Baggage Policy
category: flights
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## Hand baggage
Every passenger may bring one piece of hand baggage (maximum 55 x 40 x 23 cm, 8 kg) plus one small personal item such as a handbag or laptop bag (maximum 40 x 30 x 15 cm). Business and First passengers may bring two pieces of hand baggage of 8 kg each.

## Checked baggage allowance by fare
- Economy Light: no checked baggage included. A first bag (up to 23 kg) can be purchased.
- Economy Classic and Economy Flex: 1 checked bag of up to 23 kg.
- Business: 2 checked bags of up to 32 kg each.
- First: 3 checked bags of up to 32 kg each.
The maximum dimensions of a checked bag are 158 cm (length + width + height).

## Excess and overweight baggage
Additional bags can be purchased online up to 6 hours before departure at a lower price than at the airport. A bag weighing between 23 kg and 32 kg in Economy is charged as overweight. Bags heavier than 32 kg are not accepted as checked baggage and must be sent as cargo.

## Liquids and restricted items
Liquids in hand baggage must be in containers of no more than 100 ml, packed in one transparent resealable 1-litre bag. Lithium batteries and power banks must be carried in hand baggage, never in checked baggage.

## Sports equipment and special items
One set of ski or golf equipment can be carried free of charge in place of a regular checked bag on Economy Classic and higher fares. Bicycles, surfboards and other oversized items must be registered at least 24 hours before departure and are charged as additional baggage.

## Delayed, damaged or lost baggage
Report delayed or damaged baggage at the airport baggage service desk before leaving the arrivals hall and keep the property irregularity report (PIR). Damage must be reported in writing within 7 days and delays within 21 days of receiving the bag. Reasonable expenses for essential items during a delay are reimbursed against receipts.
