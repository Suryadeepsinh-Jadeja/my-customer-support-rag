# Flight Change Policy
category: flights
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## What can be changed
The flight and the travel dates of a ticket can be changed. The origin, the destination and the passenger name cannot be changed; these require cancelling and rebooking.

## Change fees by fare type
- Economy Light: changes are not permitted.
- Economy Classic: CHF 70 change fee per passenger and direction, plus any fare difference.
- Economy Flex: free of charge, only the fare difference applies.
- Business: CHF 100 change fee per passenger and direction, plus any fare difference.
- Business Flex / First: free of charge, only the fare difference applies.
If the new flight is cheaper, the fare difference is not refunded.

## Deadlines
Changes can be made up to 3 hours before the scheduled departure of the original flight. Tickets that are already checked in, or flights that have already departed, cannot be changed.

## Same-day changes
Economy Flex and Business Flex passengers may change to an earlier direct flight on the same day and on the same route free of charge, subject to seat availability, during online check-in or at the airport.

## Seats, meals and extras after a change
Seat reservations, special meals and passenger details (APIS) are carried over to the new flight where possible. The booking reference stays the same and a new ticket number may be issued.

## How to change a flight
Customers can ask this support assistant to change their flight after signing in. Unlike the online self-service rebooking described in the SWISS FAQ, the assistant can rebook any ticket in the signed-in customer's profile, regardless of the ticket number prefix. The assistant searches for alternative flights, explains any fee or fare difference and asks for explicit confirmation before the change is made.
