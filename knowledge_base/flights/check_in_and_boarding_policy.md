# Check-in and Boarding Policy
category: flights
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## Online check-in
Online and mobile check-in opens 23 hours before departure and closes 60 minutes before departure for European flights and 90 minutes before departure for intercontinental flights. Boarding passes can be downloaded to a phone or printed.

## Airport check-in and bag drop
Check-in counters and bag drop close 40 minutes before departure for European flights and 60 minutes before departure for intercontinental flights. Passengers who arrive after these deadlines may be refused carriage and treated as a no-show.

## Boarding
Boarding starts 30 to 45 minutes before departure. The gate closes 20 minutes before departure for European flights and 30 minutes before departure for intercontinental flights.

## Seat selection
Seats can be reserved at booking. Free seat selection is included from Economy Classic upward; on Economy Light, seats are assigned free of charge at check-in or can be reserved for a fee.

## Changes after check-in
Once a passenger has checked in, the ticket can no longer be changed online. To change a checked-in flight, the check-in must first be cancelled at the airport.
