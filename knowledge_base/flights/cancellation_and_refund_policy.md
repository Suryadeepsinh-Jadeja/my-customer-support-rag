# Flight Cancellation and Refund Policy
category: flights
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## 24-hour risk-free cancellation
Any ticket, including non-refundable fares, can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of booking, as long as the booking was made at least 7 days before the scheduled departure. The full ticket price, including taxes and fees, is refunded to the original form of payment.

## Cancellation fees by fare type
After the 24-hour window, the cancellation fee depends on the fare type of the ticket (shown as the "Fare Class" / fare conditions on the booking):
- Economy Light: non-refundable. Only government taxes and airport charges are refunded.
- Economy Classic: refundable minus a cancellation fee of CHF 150 per passenger and direction.
- Economy Flex: fully refundable until 24 hours before departure. Cancellations later than 24 hours before departure incur a CHF 100 fee.
- Business: refundable minus a cancellation fee of CHF 250 per passenger and direction.
- Business Flex / First: fully refundable at any time before departure.
In the travel database the fare conditions "Economy", "Comfort" and "Business" correspond to Economy Classic, Economy Flex and Business respectively.

## No-show
If a passenger does not show up for a flight without cancelling beforehand, the fare is not refunded and all subsequent flights in the same booking are cancelled automatically. Government taxes and airport charges can still be reclaimed on request.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds are always made to the original form of payment and in the currency in which the ticket was issued. Card refunds are normally processed within 7 business days; refunds for tickets bought through a travel agency are paid out by the travel agency. Services included in a fare that were not used (for example an unused checked bag or seat reservation) are not refunded separately.

## Partially used tickets
If only part of a return ticket has been used, the refund for the unused part is calculated as the difference between the price paid and the one-way fare for the flown segment, minus the applicable cancellation fee.

## Cancellations by the airline
If the airline cancels a flight or delays it by more than 5 hours, passengers may choose between a free rebooking on the next available flight or a full refund of the unused ticket, regardless of fare type. Under EU Regulation 261/2004, compensation may additionally apply unless the cancellation was caused by extraordinary circumstances such as severe weather, air traffic control restrictions or security risks.

## How to cancel
Customers can cancel through this support assistant after signing in. The assistant shows the ticket to be cancelled and asks for explicit confirmation before the cancellation is executed. A cancellation cannot be undone once confirmed.
