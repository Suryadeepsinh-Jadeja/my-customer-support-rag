# Travel Documents and Special Assistance
category: travel
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## Travel documents
Passengers are responsible for carrying valid travel documents for every country on their itinerary, including transit countries. The name on the ticket must match the travel document exactly. Passports should be valid for at least three months beyond the planned departure date from the Schengen area; many non-European countries require six months of validity. This assistant cannot confirm visa or entry requirements: always check with the embassy or official government sources of the destination country.

## Name corrections
Name changes are not permitted after a booking is made. Minor spelling corrections (up to three characters) can be requested through customer support before check-in.

## Reduced mobility
Passengers who need wheelchair assistance or other mobility support should request it at least 48 hours before departure. The service is free of charge at the departure, transfer and arrival airports.

## Unaccompanied minors
Children aged 5 to 11 travelling alone must use the unaccompanied minor service, which must be booked at least 48 hours before departure and is charged per direction. Children under 5 cannot travel alone.

## Pregnancy
Pregnant passengers may fly without a medical certificate up to the end of the 36th week of pregnancy (32nd week for multiple pregnancies).

## Pets
Small dogs and cats up to 8 kg including the carrier may travel in the cabin. Larger animals travel in the temperature-controlled cargo hold. Animal transport must be registered at least 48 hours before departure.
