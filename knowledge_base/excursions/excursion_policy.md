# Excursion and Trip Recommendation Policy
category: excursions
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## Recommendations
The assistant recommends excursions and activities (for example landmarks, museums, city tours and outdoor activities) based on the customer's destination and interests. Recommendations are suggestions only; an excursion is only reserved after the booking has been completed and confirmed by the customer.

## Booking and changes
Excursions can be booked up to 24 hours before the start time, subject to availability. Special requests or details (such as group size, language or accessibility needs) can be added to an existing excursion booking up to 24 hours before the start.

## Cancellation
Excursions can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the start time. Later cancellations are charged 50% of the price, and no-shows are charged in full.

## Weather and operator cancellations
Outdoor excursions may be cancelled by the operator because of weather or safety conditions. In that case the customer can choose a free rebooking to another date or a full refund.

## Accessibility
Not every excursion is accessible for travellers with reduced mobility. Customers should mention accessibility needs when booking so that suitability can be checked with the operator.
