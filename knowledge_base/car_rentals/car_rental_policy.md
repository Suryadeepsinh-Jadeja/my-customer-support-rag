# Car Rental Policy
category: car_rentals
last_updated: 2026-09-01
note: Demo policy written for this application. Not official SWISS policy.

## Booking a car rental
Car rentals offered through this assistant are provided by partner companies (for example Europcar, Avis, Hertz, Sixt and Enterprise) at Swiss and European destinations. Rentals are available in the categories Economy, Compact, Midsize, Full-size, SUV, Premium and Luxury. A rental is only confirmed after the booking has been completed and confirmed by the customer.

## Driver requirements
The main driver must be at least 21 years old and must have held a valid driving licence for at least one year. Drivers under 25 pay a young driver surcharge. Premium and Luxury vehicles require a minimum age of 25. A credit card in the main driver's name is required at pick-up.

## Changes to a rental
Pick-up and return dates can be changed free of charge up to 24 hours before pick-up, subject to availability. Price differences resulting from the change apply.

## Cancellation
Car rentals can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the pick-up time. Cancellations made later than 48 hours before pick-up incur a fee equal to one rental day. No refund is given for a no-show or for an early return.

## Fuel and mileage
Vehicles are provided with a full tank and must be returned full; otherwise refuelling is charged. Unlimited mileage is included within Switzerland and neighbouring countries.

## Insurance
Third-party liability insurance is included. Collision damage waiver (CDW) and theft protection are included with a deductible; the deductible can be reduced by purchasing additional cover at pick-up.
